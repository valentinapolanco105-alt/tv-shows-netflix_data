# tv-shows-netflix_data
Repository STA 145

My replication package for my STA-145 final project. I gathered different Netflix tv shows and analyzed qualitative variables such as Setting, the options being:
  Contemporary,
  Historical,
  Futuristic,
  Fantasy World,
  Mixed.
  Also, Production Status and the options being:
  Cancelled,
  Ongoing,
  Ended.

Instructions:
To access and utilize the script, open the "polancv1-final_project.R" file, which contains the code for my project. To access the dataset, open the "raw_data.csv" file.

 Data: 
 The data I collected for this project was popular tv shows on netflix. The goal of this analysis was to gather information in regards to whether shows were still filming or ended, and where they took place. 

 Operationalization: 
 To operationalize, I went online and checked whether the tv shows were actively filming, ended, or got cancelled. I did the same thing for setting and found where each show took place, whether it was in contemporary, historical, futuristic, fantasy world, or mixed. Shows that were contemporary typically took place in the present day or very recent past. Historical setting shows were anchored in a specific, real-world era of the past, ranging from ancient civilizations to the late 20th century. A futuristic setting projects forward into near-future or far-future timelines, often exploring advanced technology, societal shifts, or space exploration. Fantasy worlds settings introduce magic, mythical creatures, or entirely fabricated geographies. A mixed setting blends two or more of the categories above such as Science Fantasy (Futuristic + Fantasy) and more. 
 Production status was operationalized in a similar way, I searched whether shows were Cancelled, typically online it would state the reasons why the shows were cancelled, ongoing, it would mention that the shows were currently filming or planning on filming more, and ended, stating that the show reached its max seasons and their contract closed.
